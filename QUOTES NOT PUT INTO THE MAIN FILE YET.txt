"Momentum isn't the absence of whispers. It's choosing your direction before the whispers choose it for you."

You have to envision something more powerful than you. Something has to get you out of bed, and you have to create it, and it has to be false. 

Before you quit ask yourself one question, Can you take one more step? Usually the answer is yes. If you can answer that question and not another step, that is real failure that is real quitting. A lot of people can take one more step and choose not to, I don't know if you can take two steps, you have to answer that question after the first step. So take that step.
